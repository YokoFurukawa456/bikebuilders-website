Sea Otter Classic & Collegiate Bike Building Showcase

Sea Otter Classic is an annual bike exhibition where industry professionals and organizations (like BBB) participate to show off bike-related projects and products, and hold bike races. Several university teams are attending the expo alongside us, including Cal Poly, UC Irvine, University of Victoria, and Penn State. One of our goals for Sea Otter 2026 is to showcase our completed MTB with bike building teams from other universities and race it in one of the courses!

Notable participating companies and bike brands include Specialized, Marin, Canyon, Brompton, Cannondale, Continental, Bosch, and Mips. For more information on Sea Otter Classic, visit the website: seaotterclassic.com
